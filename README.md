📊 Telecom Customer Churn & Revenue Risk Analysis
An end-to-end Power BI dashboard built to analyze customer churn patterns, identify high-risk revenue segments, and provide actionable insights for customer retention strategies.

🚀 Project Overview:
Customer retention is critical for telecom businesses. This project investigates customer churn dynamics from a multi-column dataset, focusing on service subscriptions, contract types, tenure, and financial risk exposure to help executives make data-driven retention decisions.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used:
Power BI: Data visualization, interactive filtering, and dashboard layout design.

Power Query (ETL): Data transformation, cleaning, and formatting.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Custom measures for churn rate, active users, and revenue risk.

GitHub: Version control and portfolio presentation.

📈 Key Business Insights & KPIs:
Total Customers Monitored: Comprehensive tracking of active vs. churned subscriber base.

Overall Churn Rate: Identified critical drop-off points, particularly among month-to-month contract holders.

Revenue at Risk: Quantified financial loss driven by high-value customers leaving the platform.

Service Vulnerabilities: Highlighted specific services (such as lack of tech support or online security) strongly correlated with higher churn.

🖼️ Dashboard Preview & Design Highlights:
Executive Dark Theme: Styled with professional dark container backgrounds and high-contrast color palettes for reduced eye strain and impactful executive presentations.

Interactive Slicers: Seamless filtering by contract type, payment method, and tenure buckets.

Visual Storytelling: Clear layout structure featuring KPI summary cards, donut charts for demographic breakdown, and trend analysis.
